# Settings ⌘,

Ten sections in a window of their own, from Appearance to About.

## Appearance

Your avatar and name, the window’s theme, and its accent: Periwinkle, Orchid, Rose, Amber, Moss, Jade or Teal. The list at the right of the section’s name picks the window’s language, from eight. Under Power, a switch keeps the display on while a session works.

## Text

Two pages, Fonts and Size, chosen at the right of the section’s name.

### Fonts

Eight faces for the chat, four serif and four sans serif, and four monospace faces for code and the terminals.

### Size

Small, Medium and Large set every place at once. Custom is your own set: each place’s size, stepped a point at a time or typed.

## Conversation

How the chat draws the agent’s work: three or more tool calls in a row fold into one row that opens, and the [Outline](../docs/outline.llms.md) can be turned off. Then the composer: the key that sends, auto-capitalization, and spelling and grammar checked as you type, in US or UK English, Spanish, French or German. Spanish, French and German are checked on a Mac only.

## Model calls

Everything Emaki asks a small model for, each a call on your account: the plain-word explanations of tool calls, and the Outline’s labels and summaries. Each has a switch, and each agent has a model to choose for them, from its own list.

## New sessions

Each agent’s permission mode, model and effort for a new session. The pills under the composer change them for one session ([Chats](../docs/chats.llms.md#mode-model-and-effort)).

## Panels

Which side each goes on: the [Files Panel](../docs/files.llms.md) and the [Terminal Panel](../docs/terminal.llms.md), one at each side of the chat, and in its margins the [Outline](../docs/outline.llms.md)’s seam and the layout’s, which brings in the [Agent CLI](../docs/agent-cli.llms.md). Each pair is one choice, in the order they stand from left to right.

## Files

Whether a file opens as it reads or as it is written, and format on save with the languages it covers, each with its own formatter.

## Terminal

The terminal’s theme, its cursor, ligatures and copy on select. Its face is set in Fonts and its size in Size.

## Shortcuts

The keys of the window’s commands, in two lists: the window’s, Sessions and Panels, and the file editor’s Sublime Text commands, Lines and Text. Click a command’s keys and press new ones; a key already taken is refused, and a button puts a changed key back. The [Keys](../docs/keys.llms.md) page lists them as they begin.

## About

Who makes Emaki, the version, and updates: Emaki checks once a day, and installs nothing without you.

# Outline Panel ⌘⇧O

Summaries of your past prompts.

Going back through a long session is a pain. The Outline Panel lists a summary of each of your past prompts, with its time in front. As with the plain-word explanations of the agent’s tool calls, the summaries are written by the lightest model your session’s agent has.

Click an entry to scroll back to its prompt.

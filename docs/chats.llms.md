# Chats

Main panel of Emaki, with high-quality rendering and smooth UX.

## Transcript

Chat transcripts are well-rendered with high-quality interface. Agent tool calls are stacked to release reading pressure. You can expand them and even get summaries of each tool call to understand what happened. Artifacts, background jobs, subagents, Q&A sessions are all properly taken care of with smooth UI.

## Composer

Attach files by pasting, dropping, or the + button. **`@`** picks a file or folder from the project, and **`/`** lists the agent’s slash commands and skills. Spelling and grammar are checked as you type.

We proudly recommend using [Kotoba](https://github.com/alkaline-software/kotoba) as your dictation app on Emaki or any other composer on your machine. It supports light-weighted models for voice-to-text dictation and post-processing.

## Mode, model and effort

By providing three pills under the composer, Emaki made it easy to toggle among different modes, models, and efforts without needing to trigger keyboard commands or **`/`** commands (though they are also supported).

## Status line

The row under the composer shows how much of the context window is used and where the 5-hour and 7-day limits stand. Its colors change according to the thresholds. The status line logic is from the [Waza](https://github.com/tw93/waza) plugin by [tw93](https://github.com/tw93).

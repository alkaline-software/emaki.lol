A shell where the work is

Your login shell, opened in the session’s folder, at the chat’s right. Shells are tabs: the + or ⌘T opens one, ⌘W closes it, ⌘K clears the screen, and a drag reorders them. They stay open until you close them or quit.

Paste a picture and it lands as the path of a file. A selection can copy itself when you let go (Settings, Terminal). Mouse-aware programs take clicks and scrolls, and the Mac’s line keys work (⌘←, ⌘→, ⌘⌫). The look is Kaku’s, in the window’s own colors and face.

The same shell opens beside the install steps on the Agents page, for the commands they give. It shares the right side with the agent terminal: one shows at a time.

# Terminals

A shell that opens in the project’s folder, and your agent’s own CLI.

## Shell Terminal Panel ⌘⇧S

Opening a terminal and `cd`-ing to the project is easy, but doing it again and again is a pain. Emaki solves it for you! The Shell Terminal Panel gives you terminal tabs that open in the project’s folder. Use the **+** button or ⌘T to open a new tab, and ⌘W to close one. Inside the terminal, you can use your mouse, scrolling, and keyboard shortcuts. On Windows and Linux the keys are your system terminal’s: Ctrl with a letter goes to the shell, and the panel’s own keys add Shift (Ctrl⇧T, Ctrl⇧W, Ctrl⇧C, Ctrl⇧V).

Its look is inspired by [Kaku](https://kaku.fun), in the window’s own colors and font.

## Agent Terminal Panel ⌘⇧A

If you want your agent’s original CLI, this is the right panel. You can use it as a fallback, or even continue your session in it. As in the Shell Terminal Panel, you can use your mouse, scrolling, and keyboard shortcuts.
